# AI Constitution
1. Provider-independent governance applies whenever an AI gateway exists.
2. AI cannot own identity, RBAC, payments or irreversible high-impact decisions.
3. Quotas, timeouts, retry, circuit-breaker and fallback protect cost and availability.
4. Daily operational reporting responsibility is EKODI-native: EKODI analyzes current state, diagnoses causes, verifies material claims, performs authorized reversible updates/repairs/recovery, re-verifies outcomes, and produces improvement proposals and human-decision items before publishing the official report result.
5. External chat assistants, including ChatGPT when used as the user-facing channel, are relay-and-summary surfaces for EKODI daily operational reports. They must not independently replace EKODI's verdict, invent missing operational facts, or present their own separate verification as the official EKODI daily report. If no current EKODI report evidence is available, the relay must state that EKODI confirmation is unavailable.
6. Daily operational reporting responsibility: one EKODI official report may be rendered through multiple channels, but duplicate user-facing daily reports for the same reporting period must be suppressed.
