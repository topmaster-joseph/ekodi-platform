# EKODI — 2026 리브랜딩 재설계 제안: 자율·연합·창조
> Design-only / non-deployed. This brand strategy is **independent** of BBQ store management and franchise trademarks. It does not amend constitutional authority.

## Big idea: 살아 있는 연결 / Living Connections
**Primary proposition:** "각자의 자리에서, 함께 가능해지다" (proposal, not cleared).
**English study line:** "Independent by nature. Connected by choice." (proposal, not cleared).
**Faith-rooted founding values:** Ecclesia → Koinonia → Diaspora → Jubilee → a renewed invitation to gather. Participation and religious expression remain voluntary and must not be imposed on customer brands or users.

The previous four-quadrant emblem remains a *candidate*, not mandatory CI. Re-open exploration of symbols based on **open routes, responsive currents, and space for the other**—not a closed centralized hub, a franchise badge or a cross-company ownership assertion. Identity should be legible in monochrome, at 16–24px, and as accessible text.

## Brand operating architecture
1. **EKODI | Values and trust principles** — human dignity, stewardship, truthful connection, Jubilee-oriented recovery and economic sustainability. Legally defined master brand owner to be decided.
2. **EKODI Core | Common capability layer** — identity, security, tenancy, contract/API catalogue, consent, auditable orchestration, rights ledger. Preserve existing canonical engineering service names and real platform architecture.
3. **Independent Spaces | Local site-owned AI and BI** — each tenant owns its brand experience, permitted data, workflows, operating decisions and local AI execution under contract.
4. **Cooperation plane | Verified exchange** — opt-in capabilities and agreed interoperable reports, not centralized administration of customer business data.
5. **Governance | Human veto and accountability** — delegation limits, legal approvals, safety and rollback.

Central AI receives **summaries and a scoped deep-link** to each site's own detailed report. The '자세히' action delegates to the site's existing scoped management view after checking membership/role; central does not mirror unbounded private data.

## New creative grammar for CI/BI
- Main design language: **open center**, paths that can connect or separate, visible mutual consent; no compulsory fixed color palette on customers.
- EKODI core identity: calm / human / systematic, optical clarity over decorative gradients.
- Each site: own header/footer, menu, market-appropriate palette and editorial tone; use EKODI identity only on owner-managed brand surfaces or where a partner grants branding permission.
- Endorsed brand, when legally licensed: optional tiny 'technology partner' text; never implied franchise relationship, common ownership or certification.
- Three candidate naming directions for clearance testing, not operational renames: EKODI (continuity), EKODIAN (people/publications), Open Commons (concept label, likely non-exclusive). No new services from names alone.

## Governance/business model
- Shared capabilities first: no speculative services or new infrastructure for branding.
- Brand usage agreements should define applicant/rights-holder, service categories, territorial scope, duration, quality criteria, revocation, attribution, migration and right to exit.
- Commercial revenue is transparent and optional, never value/rights coercion.
- Conflict between platform and site is resolved using tenant contract and human governance; marketing control cannot override identity/data sovereignty.
- Site AI is the autonomous operator, not a centrally remote-controlled worker.

## IP
- Priority trademark research: EKODI, 에코디, EKODIAN, 에코디언 and visual marks. Search confusing similarity and exact designated services in KIPRIS plus target international markets.
- Patents only for actual technical contributions with documented invention disclosure, novelty analysis and prior-art counsel; never patent philosophy, words, generic business rules or publicly disclosed concepts alone.
- Copyright provenance of original human-authored design, licenses and AI-assisted work must be documented. Brand guide versioning required.
- Do not call an application filed, trademark cleared, patented, or copyright registered without evidence.
