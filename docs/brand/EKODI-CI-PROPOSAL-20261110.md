### Four-value flow

Ecclesia (모임·초대) → Koinonia (나눔·돌봄) → Diaspora (흩어짐·삶의 자리) → Jubilee (회복·새 출발) → Ecclesia.

The four open paths in the proposed logo leave the center intentionally unoccupied, representing agency without isolation, community without possession and a return to lived practice. This is a symbolic reading, not a claim of exclusive rights to generic shapes or concepts.

## Brand sovereignty

1. The ecosystem is a philosophy and voluntary cooperation network, **not automatically one incorporated legal person**.
2. Branding does not merge financial accounts, legal obligations, personal data or tenant access.
3. Each site may retain its independent logo, header, footer, name and customer presentation.
4. Optional common identity use requires the local site authority and the master-brand rights holder's approved policy.
5. Prior user-facing naming convention remains: do not prefix all specialist AI/service names with '에코디' by default.
6. Changes to production sites, routing, auth, governance and customer branding are **out of scope** for this design-only PR.
